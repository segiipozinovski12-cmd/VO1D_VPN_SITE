# Third-party data sources

## Au1rxx/free-vpn-subscriptions

VO1D can optionally consume the public country subscription outputs from:

https://github.com/Au1rxx/free-vpn-subscriptions

The upstream project is MIT licensed. Its software copyright/license remains with its respective author.

VO1D does not claim ownership or operation of the public relay nodes contained in that feed. The optional feature is branded **VO1D Community** in the client UI so it is visually distinct from VO1D-operated infrastructure.

The upstream project itself warns that public/free relay operators can observe traffic and should not be trusted for banking, important logins, or other sensitive use. VO1D preserves that distinction in the bot.
